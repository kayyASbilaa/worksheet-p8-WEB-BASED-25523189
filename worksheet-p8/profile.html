Book Categories

The books in my personal collection are divided into several categories. Each category has its own characteristics and provides a different reading experience.

Self-Development

Self-Development books focus on personal growth, self-improvement, motivation, productivity, and building positive habits. These books can help readers develop themselves and improve their daily lives.

Atomic Habits, The Art of Noticing, and Is it Better to speak or die? are included in this category in my collection.

Classic Fiction

Classic Fiction books contain fictional stories that have remained popular and meaningful over time. These books often discuss human relationships, society, morality, and different aspects of life.

To Kill a Mockingbird by Harper Lee is one of the classic fiction books in my personal collection.

Other Books

Other Books is a category for books that do not directly belong to the Self-Development or Classic Fiction categories. This category can contain books with different genres, topics, and purposes.
